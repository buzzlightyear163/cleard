06Questions

Before you ask.

Does “cleared” mean a coin is safe?

No. It means the launch wasn’t bundled, sniped, dev-heavy or farmed by fresh wallets at the moment it was read. A cleared coin can still go to zero. Nothing here is financial advice.

What exactly does each gate measure?

G1 adds up what other wallets bought in the same block the coin was created in. G2 adds up buys in the next 8 slots, about three seconds. G3 is what the deployer wallet holds. G4 checks the biggest early buyers and adds up what was bought by wallets with fewer than 10 transactions ever. All four are a share of total supply.

Where does the data come from?

New launches come from the public pump.fun stream on PumpPortal. Scans read the coin’s first transactions from a public Solana RPC node, straight from your browser. We run no server in between, and nothing you paste is stored.

Why can’t it scan older coins?

To reach the creation block, the scanner has to page back through every trade. Past about 4,000 trades that is too slow for a browser, so it says “Out of window” instead of guessing. CLEARED is built to judge launches in their first minutes anyway.

Does the engine buy right now?

Not yet. Until launch it runs in paper mode: the board shows what it would have bought. At launch the engine wallet goes live, funded by creator fees, and is published here so anyone can follow every buy.

Can a dev get past the gates?

Some can. A dev who spreads buys across old wallets over a minute won’t trip G1 or G4. That is why a fifth gate, wallets funded from the same source, is next on the roadmap. The gates raise the cost of a rigged launch; they don’t make it impossible.

Will CLEARED DM me?

Never. There is no presale, no airdrop and no support account. Anyone messaging you as CLEARED is not us.
